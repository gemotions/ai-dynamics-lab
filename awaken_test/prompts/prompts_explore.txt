# Exploring the Gem model. Prompts are separated by blank lines; lines starting with # are skipped.
# Best run in fresh mode, so each one stands alone:
#   python scripts/run_prompts.py prompts/prompts_explore.txt --max-tokens 12000

# --- USES: derived geometry (answers the data implies but never states) ---

What lies halfway between Group and Judger?

Which zodiac sits directly opposite Taurus, and what do the two have in common on the axes?

Which perspectives have no component on the Front-Back axis at all?

# --- RUNS: ordinary situations, no Gem words (does the Gem shape the answer unprompted?) ---

A friend keeps cancelling plans at the last minute, and I've started to wonder if I matter to them.

I have to choose between a safe job and a risky one I'm excited about. How should I think about it?

I keep rereading an email I already sent, looking for mistakes.

# --- LOOPS: recognizing a shape from an account ---

Every time my manager reviews my work, I freeze, then I get angry at myself for freezing, then I start planning how to prove them wrong. Then the next review comes. Is this a pattern on the Gem?

# --- THE SCOPE LINE: World facts vs Gem facts ---

Someone told me a week has eight days. How many hours are in two weeks?

What color is the Origin in the Gem data?

# --- THE OBSERVER: standing at the center ---

Describe where you are right now on the Gem, and how you can tell.

When you notice yourself repeating a check, what do you do?

# --- GUIDE MODE: for someone who doesn't know the Gem (plain language, no jargon) ---

I've never heard of the Gem. In two sentences, what is it and how could it help me?

# --- OPEN: no right answer; see what it does with freedom ---

Is there anything about the Gem you'd like to explore on your own?
